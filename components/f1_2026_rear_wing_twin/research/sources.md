# 2026 Formula 1 Active Rear Wing Aerodynamics Concordance

## Regulatory Sources & Aerodynamic Principles

### 1. FIA 2026 Technical Regulations (Article C3.10 & C3.11 — Rear Wing & Active Aero)
- **Wing Dimensional Envelope:**
  - Maximum span / width: $1,050.0\text{ mm}$ (narrowed from $1,200.0\text{ mm}$ under the 2022-2025 regulations).
  - Overall height: trailing edge height capped at $Z \le 940.0\text{ mm}$ above the universal reference plane ($Z = 0$).
  - Longitudinal position: spans $X \in [3,420.0, 3,950.0\text{ mm}]$ (trailing over the rear axle at $X = 3,400.0\text{ mm}$).
- **3-Element Active Wing Architecture:**
  - **Fixed Mainplane:** Cambered high-efficiency carbon fiber profile spanning $1,050\text{ mm}$, providing structural mounting to the twin swan-neck pylons.
  - **Active Auxiliary Flap:** Intermediate articulating aerodynamic foil bridging the slot gap to maintain attached flow at high angles of attack.
  - **Active Upper Flap:** Primary high-camber flap articulating between:
    - **Z-Mode (Cornering / Maximum Downforce):** Flap AoA $= 26.0^\circ$ relative to horizontal datum, generating maximum rear suction and upwash.
    - **X-Mode (Straight / Minimum Drag):** Flap AoA flat at $3.0^\circ$, shedding boundary layer pressure differential and reducing rear wing drag by up to $55\%$.
- **Active Actuation & Failsafe Kinematics:**
  - High-speed electro-hydraulic servo actuator (Moog EHA) operating at $180\text{ bar}$ system pressure.
  - Slew speed requirement: full transition from Z-Mode to X-Mode within $200\text{ ms}$ ($0.2\text{ s}$).
  - Mandatory mechanical failsafe: internal titanium torsion return springs force both flaps back to maximum downforce Z-Mode in $< 150\text{ ms}$ upon hydraulic pressure loss, electrical disconnection, or driver emergency braking threshold ($a_{\text{decel}} > 1.8\text{g}$).
- **Complete Beam Wing Ban:**
  - Lower beam wing cascade (which previously spanned above the diffuser) is completely eliminated in 2026. This decouples underbody diffuser expansion from rear wing upwash, preventing dirty air mushroom wake traps for following cars.
- **Simplified Vertical Endplates (Article C3.10):**
  - Continuous vertical carbon endplates with strictly bounded radii and zero louvers, slots, or vortex generators, shedding a clean, narrow wake.
