# 2026 Formula 1 Unified Front End Assembly Concordance

## Overview
This twin represents the unified mechanical, structural, and aerodynamic integration of the entire front quarter of the 2026 Formula 1 car ("Nimble Car" regulations). It unifies:
1. **Master Survival Cell Monocoque** (`f1_2026_monocoque_twin`): Chassis Datum $[0,0,0]$, Bulkhead A-A, titanium Halo ($125\text{ kN}$ proof), primary roll hoop ($172\text{ kN}$ proof), and pedal box sled.
2. **Active Front Wing & Two-Stage FIS** (`f1_2026_front_wing_twin`): $1,900\text{ mm}$ inwash wing, Stage 1/2 crash cones ($101.25\text{ kJ}$ absorption), and active articulating flaps ($24^\circ$ Z-Mode to $6^\circ$ X-Mode).
3. **Front Suspension & Steering Linkage** (`f1_2026_suspension_twin`): Aerodynamic carbon wishbones with $14.2^\circ$ anti-dive rake, carbon pull-rod, inboard titanium bellcranks, and 4x $7.0\text{ kJ}$ braided Zylon tethers.
4. **Front Brake Corner Assembly** (`f1_2026_brake_twin`): 5-axis CNC titanium upright, $\varnothing 345\text{ mm} \times 34\text{ mm}$ carbon disc with 1,400+ chevron holes, Al-Li 2099 monobloc 6-piston caliper, floating bobbins, and 18-inch BBS magnesium racing wheel.
