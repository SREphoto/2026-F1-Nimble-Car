# 2026 Formula 1 Front Brake Research & OEM Technical Specifications
## OEM Lineage: Brembo Racing & AP Racing Regulatory Concordance

**FIA 2026 Technical Regulations Reference:**
- **Article C5.1 & C5.2:** Friction brake dimensional envelopes.
- **Article C11.3:** Brake-by-Wire (BBW) fail-safe mechanical continuity and rear hydraulic modulation limits.

---

## 1. Disc Geometry & Cooling Hole Physics
* **Brembo / AP Racing F1 Technical Standard:**
  * Discs are machined from needle-felt polyacrylonitrile (PAN) carbon precursors, densified over a 9-month chemical vapor infiltration (CVI) furnace cycle to achieve a density of $\approx 1.78\text{ g/cm}^3$.
  * Outer diameter: $345.0\text{ mm}$ (front maximum).
  * Thickness: $34.0\text{ mm}$ (front maximum).
  * Laser micro-drilled ventilation holes: $1,400$ holes distributed in a 5-row chevron pattern with $2.5\text{ mm}$ diameter. At $300\text{ km/h}$, each front rotor acts as a centrifugal blower expelling $>0.18\text{ kg/s}$ of air.

---

## 2. Lithium-Aluminum 2099 Monobloc Calipers
* Calipers are machined from a single billet of Al-Li 2099-T83, offering a 10% increase in elastic modulus ($E = 79\text{ GPa}$) over traditional 7075-T6 aluminum at an 8% lower density ($\rho = 2.63\text{ g/cm}^3$).
* Caliper bridge stiffness must keep flex below $0.08\text{ mm}$ under $180\text{ bar}$ line pressure.
* Differential bore sizing ($\varnothing 27, 32, 38\text{ mm}$) counters friction-induced pad taper wear.

---

## 3. Thermal Conductance & Castellated Piston Barriers
* Contact face of each titanium piston is castellated with 10 radial teeth, decreasing pad contact area by 60% and enabling forced air cooling between the pad backing plate and piston crown.
* Brake fluid boiling point (Castrol SRF / Brembo HTC 64T): $310^\circ\text{C}$ dry, $270^\circ\text{C}$ wet. Fluid operating temperatures are maintained strictly below $220^\circ\text{C}$.
