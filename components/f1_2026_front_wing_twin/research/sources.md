# 2026 Formula 1 Active Front Wing & FIS Regulatory Concordance

## Governing Regulations
* **FIA 2026 Formula 1 Technical Regulations — Section C: Aerodynamic Components**:
  * **Article C3 (Front Aerodynamics & Wing Assembly)**:
    * Geometric Reference Volume `RV-FW-PROFILES`: Maximum span $1,900\text{ mm}$ ($Y \in [-950, +950\text{ mm}]$), longitudinal overhang $X_F \in [-1250, -475\text{ mm}]$ ($775\text{ mm}$ chord footprint), vertical bounds $Z \in [60, 300\text{ mm}]$.
    * Maximum of 3 elements in any longitudinal cross-section (Element 1: Mainplane, Element 2: Intermediate Flap, Element 3: Active Upper Flap).
    * Minimum concave radius $R \ge 50\text{ mm}$.
    * Slot gap limits: $10\text{--}15\text{ mm}$ gap, $1\text{--}5\text{ mm}$ overlap.
    * Slot gap separators: maximum 3 to 4 brackets per side, maximum chord $\le 40\text{ mm}$, thickness $\le 6\text{ mm}$, alignment within $\pm 10^\circ$ of vehicle centerline.
  * **Article C3.6 (Active Aerodynamics System - Front Flap Control)**:
    * Articulation authority: Up to 2 elements permitted to rotate. Angular range $\Delta \theta \approx 10^\circ\text{--}16^\circ$ depitching from $\alpha = 24^\circ$ (Z-Mode) down to $\alpha = 6^\circ$ (X-Mode).
    * Actuation time: Full transition $\le 400\text{ ms}$ nominal, maximum legal timeout $\le 600\text{ ms}$.
    * Bilateral symmetry requirement: $|\Delta \theta| \le 1.5^\circ$ between LH and RH flaps. Violation triggers automatic SECU safety abort to Z-Mode within $\le 300\text{ ms}$.
    * Fail-safe mechanism: Mechanical return springs pre-loaded to snap flaps closed into Z-Mode upon loss of hydraulic or electrical power.
  * **Article C3.7 (Front Wing Endplates & Diveplanes)**:
    * Inwash geometry mandated to reduce outer wheel wake dispersion.
    * Micro diveplane envelope: maximum lateral projection $\le 60\text{ mm}$, edge radius $\ge 15\text{ mm}$, thickness $\ge 5\text{ mm}$. Footplates banned.
  * **Article C13 (Front Impact Structure - FIS)**:
    * Two-stage frontal impact attenuator tested with $900\text{ kg}$ trolley at $15.0\text{ m/s}$ ($54.0\text{ km/h}$), absorbing $101.25\text{ kJ}$ total energy.
    * Stage 1 (Forward Crush Cone): absorbs $40\text{--}45\text{ kJ}$.
    * Stage 2 (Secondary Survival Structure): crush force resistance $\ge 500\text{ kN}$ abutting Bulkhead A-A.
    * 4x M14 titanium mounting spigots with $>350\text{ kN}$ tensile pull-off resistance.
