# bacinger/f1-circuits files

`es-2026.geojson` (Madring, Madrid) is copied unchanged from https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits
at commit 394d8fbe70ef2c0b0c8d23ff7bee61fa09606055 (5 February 2026).

Licence: MIT, Copyright (c) Tomislav Bacinger.

It is a hand-drawn line with about one point every 50 m. Its own stated length (5474 m) is an early figure;
the line measures 5430 m against the FIA 2026 length of 5414 m. It is used only because OpenStreetMap does not yet
map the whole Madring lap as a race track.

Download again with `python3 tools/track/research/fetch_tumftm.py`.
