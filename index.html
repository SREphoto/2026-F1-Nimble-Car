Design System & Inspiration

Human Creativity & F1 Digital Twin UI Principles

Design Philosophy: Human Craftsmanship over AI Homogeneity

Inspired by the principles outlined in Punit Chawla's curated guide "10 New Websites For Design Inspiration With Real Human Creativity", this application is engineered to resist generic, cookie-cutter AI aesthetics. Instead, it prioritizes tactile micro-interactions, high-contrast technical legibility, and authentic motorsport physics and telemetry aesthetics.

Core Principles Implemented in This System

  • 1. Semantic Design Tokens over Hardcoding: Colors, surface tones, and glows are driven by dynamic variables (var(--team-primary), var(--team-accent-glow)), giving instant theming without page reload.
  • 2. Optical Contrast & Anti-Glare Dark Mode: Built on high-performance dark backgrounds (#090d14) to reduce eye fatigue and make high-energy racing colors pop with optical balance.
  • 3. Tabular Numeral Stability: Speed, RPM, gear, and telemetry numbers use monospace font metrics so changing numbers never cause layout shift or visual jitter.
  • 4. Synchronized Multi-Sensory Digital Twin: Switching team theme instantly updates the UI, the PCU-8D steering wheel display, and the physical 3D car bodywork paint in Three.js simultaneously.
